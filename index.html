About me

Bringing Manipuri to research and language technology

I am a professional Manipuri linguist specializing in translation and transcription. I work with academic institutions, language consultancies, and global platforms, delivering accurate and high-quality linguistic data in Manipuri, English, Hindi, and Korean, including translation into the Meitei Mayek script.

My recent work spans speech data recording and validation, NLP annotation for IIT Guwahati, and subtitle translation for YouTube. My research on multilingual machine translation for Northeastern Indian languages appears at WMT 2026.

  • Preserving the Manipuri language and Meitei Mayek script
  • Machine translation for low-resource languages
  • Corpus development and linguistic data
